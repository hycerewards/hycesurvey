How to Earn on HYCE Elite

Step-by-Step Guide

1. Create your account and log in. After signing up, use your login details to access the dashboard and start earning.
2. Complete tasks from the dashboard. Open any available task, follow the instructions, wait for the required time, and then verify completion.
3. Join the referral page task. New users must complete the referral task on the referral page before they can withdraw earnings.
4. Wait for the withdrawal cycle. Payments are made every 30 days from your sign-up date. Your earnings become eligible based on that schedule.
5. Keep earning and stay active. Complete more tasks, maintain your streak, and check the dashboard regularly for updates and new offers.
Important: To withdraw, new users must first complete the referral task in the referral page. Also note that payout timing starts counting from your sign-up date, and payments are processed every 30 days.

Quick Rules

• Complete tasks carefully and wait for verification.

• Use the referral page task as required for withdrawal eligibility.

• Keep your account active and avoid skipping instructions.

• Withdrawals are processed on the 30-day cycle from your registration date.
